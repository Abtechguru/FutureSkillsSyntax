# Future Syntax — Landing Page Visual Assets

## Purpose
The landing page uses **illustrative, freely licensed stock photography** to communicate digital learning, belonging and positive guidance. These photographs **do not depict Future Syntax participants**, enrolled children, or actual programme activities. Do not replace this notice with implied impact claims.

## Operational notes
- Images use descriptive alt text, intrinsic dimensions, `loading="lazy"` below the fold and high-priority loading for the hero.
- Photography is external and requires an internet connection. If a permanent offline-ready asset package is needed, download approved originals under the license and commit optimized WebP/AVIF copies through an appropriately configured binary asset workflow.
- Preserve the visible disclosure: **Photographs are illustrative stock imagery and do not depict Future Syntax programme participants.**
- Never publish real photographs of children from programme sessions without specific, documented guardian authorization and safeguarding review. Even authorized photography must follow data-minimization and retention policies.
- Avoid fake impact statistics, simulated achievements or invented participant testimonials.
